Speak and Seek — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Speak and Seek collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. Your child's speech is processed on the device and is not stored or transmitted, unless you — the parent — turn on an optional feature that says otherwise in plain language.

What the app processes on your device

  • Microphone audio while your child says a practice word. Recognition runs on the device (Apple's speech framework; on-device mode is requested whenever available). The recording is deleted immediately after scoring — and any leftover from a crash is deleted at the next launch — unless you turn on clip saving below.
  • Practice history (words attempted, results, streaks, settings) is stored in a local database on your device only. Deleting a profile removes every record and every saved clip for that child. The Parent Zone also has a "Delete all app data" option that resets the app completely, and deleting the app removes everything.

Optional features you control (all off by default)

  • Save practice clips: keeps short recordings of practice words on your device so you can replay them from the parent dashboard. Capped at the best and the most recent clip per word; older clips are deleted automatically. Never uploaded.
  • High-accuracy mode (Azure): if you enter your own Microsoft Azure Speech key, attempt audio is sent to your Azure resource for phoneme-level scoring, governed by Microsoft's terms and your Azure configuration. The key is stored in the device keychain. Turn it off any time.
  • Sharing reports: the weekly PDF and CSV export leave the device only when you tap share, via the iOS share sheet, to a destination you choose.
  • Notifications: practice reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. Nothing is pushed from a server — there is no server.

In-app purchases

The optional "Speak and Seek Unlimited" upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system, reachable only behind the parental gate. Purchase processing is handled by Apple and by RevenueCat (our purchase-management provider), which receives a random app-scoped identifier and purchase state — never your child's name, speech, audio, or practice data. No purchase is required to use the app's free content.

In-app currency (coins and shards) is earned only and can never be bought with money. There are no consumable purchases of any kind.

Children's privacy

Speak and Seek is designed for children and complies with COPPA principles by architecture: it collects nothing, transmits nothing, contains no ads or third-party analytics, and has no external links outside the parental gate.
